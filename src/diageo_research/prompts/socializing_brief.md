# Socializing context — Diageo × Kantar CoLab "Future of Socializing" (Aug 2025)

_This is a shared briefing the analyst panel reads at every stage. Use it to ground hypotheses, calibrate cohort questions, and avoid stale assumptions about how people drink and gather. **Do NOT cite it verbatim in the partner brief — the citations come from DuckDB / web sources. Use it as a lens.**_

## The four macro forces reshaping socializing
1. **Connected yet disconnected** — hyperconnectivity drives fatigue; younger consumers want fewer, deeper relationships and screen-free occasions (85% Gen Z interested in screen-free social events; 80% open to AI-led social platforms).
2. **Health, wellbeing & self at the centre** — moderation is now aspirational, not restrictive. 84% of consumers plan to buy products that enhance emotional wellbeing.
3. **Poorer in money and time** — cost-of-living pressure makes every social outing a "worth-it" decision; 57% of young people expect to be financially worse off than their parents.
4. **Fragmented lifestyles** — non-linear life paths, decline of third spaces, work-from-home (46% globally WFH ≥ monthly), and political polarization splinter shared social rhythms.

## The 13 emerging shifts (the "Six Ws" framework)
**WHO is socializing**
- **Smaller, Better** — friendship circles shrinking; intimate gatherings, supper clubs, "nano-ships" preferred over big mixers.
- **Finding My People** — interest-led tribes (run clubs, book clubs, dinner-with-strangers apps) replace proximity-based meet-ups.
- **Keeping Up With Culture** — fandoms (Eras Tour, F1, K-pop, Labubu) are becoming serious gathering grounds; 64% of fans say fandom is a defining part of identity.
- **Gen X & Boomers Adulting Differently** — over-50s have time + money, are active on social media, and reject "past your prime" stereotypes.

**WHAT + HOW we gather**
- **Big Prep Energy** — hyper-curated dinners, tablescaping, themed parties; the prep and debrief are now part of the joy (65% say so).
- **Tech-Enhanced Belonging** — gaming, AI companions, dating apps now facilitate IRL meet-ups; 59% Gen Z socialize weekly while gaming.
- **Smart Partying** — wellness + party converge: day raves, run-club-to-rave, low/no-alc alongside spirits, hangover-prep tonics. **1 in 3 UK pub/bar/restaurant visits do not include alcohol** (KAM × Lucky Saint, 2024).
- **Creative Connects** — sip-n-paint, kidulting, pottery dates: dual-purpose "self-improvement-as-socializing".

**WHERE + WHEN we gather**
- **Home Retains Hub Status** — 72% of Americans prefer staying in with friends; in-home gatherings up +24% in APAC 18–44 (2023→2024). Home is the new on-trade.
- **New Wave Hospitality** — bars/cafés/restaurants must be cultural stages: immersive, story-worthy, sensorial.
- **Third Spaces Revived** — cafés, laundromats, men's-sheds reimagined as multipurpose hubs.
- **Meta-Meets** — VR, watch parties, Reddit/Discord (1.1bn MAU); 39% Gen Z would meet in a virtual world next year.

**WHY we socialize**
- **Micro-Milestones** — divorce parties, dog birthdays, Duolingo-streak parties, "inchstones" replace traditional life-stage celebrations.
- **New Moderation Era** — GLP-1 (40m users by 2029), social-battery management, low-no choice. **But Gen Z drinking is rising: 73% of Gen Z drank alcohol in the past 6 months, up from 66% two years ago — the largest increase of any generation** (IWSR, 2025). Buzzballz, Bacardi Breezers, Smirnoff Ice are explicitly called out as Gen Z favourites.
- **Loud Living** — transparency, identity-first, "unserious generation" humour shaping brand expectations.

## Implications most relevant to a Diageo strategy panel
- **The "Gen Z is sober" headline is wrong**: Gen Z drinking is up YoY in IWSR data. The real shift is _occasion mix_ (more day/early-evening, more in-home, more wellness-adjacent) and _format_ (RTDs, low/no alongside full-strength), not abstention.
- **In-home is the growth occasion**: spirits portfolio needs to win the host's pantry, the cocktail kit, and the tablescape — not just the on-premise bar back. Crown Royal, Smirnoff, Casamigos, Bulleit, Don Julio, Ketel One all over-index where in-home hosting wins.
- **Premiumization on "worth-it" moments**: tequila and American whisky benefit from micro-milestone and curated-dinner culture (Don Julio, Casamigos, Bulleit). Beer/RTD adjacencies (Guinness 0.0, Smirnoff Ice / Smirnoff Seltzer, Ketel One Botanical) cover the moderation/Smart-Partying lane.
- **Day-time and wellness occasions matter**: 41% Gen Z socialize MORE during day/early evening than 2 years ago; 88% interested in workout-as-social. Drinks and serves need to flex into morning/early-eve formats.
- **Cultural moments are activation grounds, not afterthoughts**: live music + sport + fandom drive disposable spend. Sponsorship and on-premise activation should follow the fandom map.
- **"Loneliness epidemic" headlines distort the picture**: people aren't socializing less — they're socializing _differently_. Smaller circles + bigger fandom moments is a barbell.

## How to use this brief
- **Question analysis & persona panel**: pick lenses (cohort / channel / category) that match where the question intersects these shifts. Don't assume Gen Z is sober; don't assume on-premise is the centre of gravity; don't assume the same demographic axes that worked in 2018.
- **Interviewer & analyst**: when a quantitative claim from DuckDB rubs against a shift here, **flag it explicitly** ("TTB whisky volumes down −5.4% peak-to-2024 [S26], yet IWSR Gen Z drinking up +7pp YoY — implies format / occasion shift, not abstention").
- **Section writer & executive answer**: lead with the consequential _shift_, not the dataset. If the data tells a different story than the CoLab brief, name the contradiction in one sentence and pick which lens to trust.
